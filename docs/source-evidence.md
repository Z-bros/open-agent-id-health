## Evidence discipline
- Do not claim a proportion of patients who fail to attend a test without an appropriate denominator.
- Do not call a missing lab message a missed test, failed treatment, or patient nonadherence.
- Report synthetic demonstration results separately from actual Indonesian healthcare outcomes.
- Public WHO aggregate TB datasets are available at https://www.who.int/teams/global-tuberculosis-programme/data ; no patient-level dataset is assumed public.
